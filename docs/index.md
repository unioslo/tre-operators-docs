		<h1>The EOSC-ENTRUST guide for building interoperable TRE services</h1>
		<p class="tre-lead">Welcome to this online guide that aims to support providers of trusted research environments in building interoperable services according to the <a href="https://github.com/paalsat/entrust-blueprint">EOSC-ENTRUST Blueprint and Interoperability Framework</a>. The EOSC-ENTRUST project aims to create <strong>a European network of Trusted Research Environments (TREs) for sensitive data</strong> and drive European interoperability by joint development of a common blueprint for federated data access and analysis.</p>

		<aside style="background: #eaf3fb; border-left: 4px solid #005095; padding: 1rem 1.4rem; margin: 1rem 0; border-radius: 8px; font-size: 0.95rem; line-height: 1.6;">
			<strong>EOSC-ENTRUST</strong> stands for "European Open Science Cloud – European Network of TRUSTed Research Environments".
		</aside>

		<p class="tre-lead">This guide is set up to accompany the <a href="https://github.com/paalsat/entrust-blueprint">EOSC-ENTRUST Blueprint and Interoperability Framework</a> to guide TRE providers in building and/or adjusting their infrastructures technologically and organisationally to enable and allow exchange of data and workflows across TRE boundaries and national borders. On these pages, you will find guides covering:</p>
		<ul class="tre-lead-list">
			<li>The standard architecture of a TREs and a description of its core components (<a href="fundamentals/">Module 1</a>)</li>
			<li>Template legal agreements necessary to allow federation of TREs (<a href="modules/legal/">Module 2</a>)</li>
			<li>The Blueprint architecture for a federated network of TREs, suggested requirements (<a href="modules/architecture/">Module 3</a>)</li>
			<li>Federation interface definitions and collaboration requirements allowing TREs to connect and data and workflows to be exchanged (<a href="modules/interfaces/">Module 4</a>)</li>
			<li>Operating procedures for a federation of TREs (<a href="modules/procedures/">Module 5</a>)</li>
		</ul>
<p class="tre-lead">In addition, you will find a <a href="appendices/glossary/">Glossary</a> and <a href="appendices/reference-terminology-dictionary/">Reference Dictionary</a> of terms relevant for federated TREs, and links to relevant existing training material that may be of further help and guidance for TRE users, providers and other interested parties.</p>
